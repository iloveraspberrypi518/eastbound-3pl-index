    About

    Eastbound 3PL Index helps retailers that import into the US East Coast choose a route, a shipping mode and a 3PL. It's an early prototype. Transit times are typical estimates, route costs are a relative index, and mode-advisor rates are illustrative until real quotes are added.

    How to use it

    1. Route planner: choose a factory region, 3PL, final stop and ship month. Switch news and weather scenarios on or off. The best route and two alternatives are highlighted on the graph and the map. The crowd-effect panel shows how other shippers' choices change your best route.
    2. Mode advisor: choose a sector, weight, value and deadline to see whether air, a shared container or a full container is cheapest. It uses the route planner's scenario.
    3. 3PL index: filter providers by type or port. Use "Route it" to send one to the route planner.
    4. Ports: 2025 volumes for East Coast ports.
